\paragraph{Scope of Reproducibility.}
Diffusion Forcing claims stable video rollout beyond the training horizon, improved Maze2D planning and a reweighted ELBO \cite{chen2025diffusion}. We evaluate the released main implementation and expand the proof. All eight Minecraft schedule/horizon conditions and 14 Maze2D runs are complete. Franka robotics and time series are excluded.
\paragraph{Methodology.}
We use upstream commit \texttt{475e0bcab87545e48b24b39fb46a81fe59d80594}, released checkpoints and inference-only instrumentation on an RTX 4090 Laptop GPU with 16 GB VRAM. Each video condition has eight clips at seed zero; each maze run has 128 episodes. Default maze guidance has three model seeds with fixed environment seed zero. No training was performed. Resource totals include the interrupted video attempt and are reported in Section 3.
\paragraph{Results.}
At 80 frames, temporal LPIPS changes from 0.1339 to 0.1366 for autoregressive sampling. For full-sequence chunk-two sampling it changes from 0.1468 to 0.1421. Sampled frames show recognizable scenes without gross intensity collapse, but contain geometry and object discontinuities. Full-sequence sampling is about twice as fast. Default-guidance raw maze reward is $122.72\pm3.31$ (medium) and $139.42\pm6.78$ (large), with SD across three seed means. Higher guidance generally improves reward. Changed protocols prevent direct comparison with paper scores. The theoretical audit identifies sign and indexing discrepancies in the printed appendix.
\paragraph{What was easy.}
The released checkpoints produced finite outputs once dependencies and data were available. Paper source enabled checking figures, configurations and proof steps; measured tables and figures are generated by scripts.
\paragraph{What was difficult.}
Legacy MuJoCo required local headers and compiler compatibility flags. Released attention/sliding-window video sampling differs from the paper's RNN; planning metrics and settings also changed and MCG is absent. One interrupted run needed restarting. Video repeats exceeded the predefined resource threshold, limiting schedule conclusions.
